Here is a collection of RAW photo samples from various cameras I have used or am currently using. The purpose of this page is to provide raw file examples to you before deciding to buy a particular camera. Additionally, these RAW samples are also useful for those of you who enjoy editing or color grading with RAW photos from various brands and models.

If you find this page helpful and want to contribute your own files, I would be more than happy to receive and include them on this page.

You can send the files via email to **hello (at) rullypratama.com**. Make sure to also include the camera brand and model. Note: please ensure the contributed files are not bound by copyright, as I am not responsible for any copyright infringement. Thank you.
